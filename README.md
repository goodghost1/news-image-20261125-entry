A one-page newspaper front page for Wednesday 25 November 2026: 48 verified
stories, every one dated that day. Three heroes lead the page — Asia's chip
rout (Kospi -6.2%, Nikkei -2.8%), the Houthi strike on Ras Tanura, and
Shenzhou-24 lifting off with the first foreign astronaut — followed by ten
numbered stories, a "THE DAY IN UTC" timeline, a "WHERE IT HAPPENED" map, a
"THE DAY IN NUMBERS" panel, and a DATE CHECK box listing news from the wrong
day that was left out.

`entry.png` (1024x1024) is rendered in advance by `gen.py` from
`stories-20261125.json` and shipped in this repository. `main.py` only
validates the shipped image and copies it to the output directory; it never
draws, downloads, or regenerates anything.
